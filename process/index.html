How It Works

Five stages, clearly defined

Each stage has a clear purpose, what we need from you, and what you receive.

StageWhat happensYou provideYou receive
01Share Your Requirement

Tell us about your research area, current stage and the kind of support you are looking for through the consultation form or a short call.

You provideResearch area, stage, deadline and any university guidelines
You receiveA reply from a consultant and a short discovery call
02Research Assessment

A consultant reviews your brief, existing drafts or data and identifies where structured support will make the most difference.

You provideExisting drafts, data or supervisor feedback, if available
You receiveAn assessment of where structured support will help most
03Customized Support Plan

You receive a written scope with deliverables, timeline and the specialists involved, so expectations are clear before work begins.

You provideConfirmation of priorities and timeline
You receiveA written scope with deliverables, milestones and specialists
04Expert Review & Assistance

Work proceeds in agreed milestones with documented feedback, review rounds and regular progress updates.

You provideWork in progress and your feedback on each review
You receiveDocumented reviews, analysis and agreed revision rounds
05Final Delivery / Guidance

You receive the final deliverables along with guidance on next steps, whether that is submission, revision or publication.

You provideFinal approval of the deliverables
You receiveFinal files and guidance on submission, revision or publication

Our Commitments

What applies to every engagement

  • Confidential Research Handling

    Drafts, data and personal details stay private and are deleted on request.

  • Structured Research Process

    Defined stages with clear deliverables, so support is predictable and reviewable.

  • Personalized Support

    Scope, depth and pace of support tailored to your stage and your institution.

  • Transparent Communication

    Written scope, agreed timelines and honest advice about what is achievable.

We provide research support and guidance. We do not guarantee publication, grades or degree outcomes; those decisions rest with journals and your institution.
