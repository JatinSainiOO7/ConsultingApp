Working With Us

Confidential, clear and on schedule

What happens to your information, and what to expect from the first message to the final handover.

Your data stays confidential

  • Your personal details, drafts, data and code are seen only by the consultants on your engagement.
  • Nothing you share is reused, published or passed to anyone else.
  • A signed confidentiality agreement is available on request.
  • Your files are deleted on request when the project closes.

What to expect

  • Written scope. Before work starts, you receive a scope that lists the deliverables and what each stage includes.
  • Agreed timeline. Every milestone has a delivery date that we agree with you at the start.
  • Progress updates. Your consultant updates you at each milestone, by email or WhatsApp, and whenever something changes.
  • Clear handover. Final files come with notes, so you can explain and defend the work.

Revision Policy

Revisions and corrections

Every service includes revision rounds. The terms for your project are written into your scope before work starts.

  • Revision roundsChanges that you or your supervisor ask for on a delivered item, within the agreed window.
  • CorrectionsErrors on our side are corrected at no extra cost.
  • New scopeNew chapters, analyses or experiments are quoted separately before any work starts.

Our Ethics

What we do, and what we don't

Clear boundaries protect your degree and our integrity.

We do

  • Guide research design, analysis and interpretation
  • Implement and explain data analysis and AI code with you
  • Prepare manuscripts and submission packages with you
  • Prepare conference papers and presentations with you

We don't

  • Write theses, chapters or papers for submission as your own
  • Submit work to journals on your behalf
  • Guarantee publication, grades or degree outcomes
  • Share, reuse or publish your data and drafts
  • Confidential Data and drafts never shared.
  • Author-first Your research stays yours.
  • Written scope Deliverables agreed upfront.
  • Responsive Clear milestones and updates.
