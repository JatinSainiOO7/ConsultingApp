Deliverables and revisions

What you receive, and how changes are handled.

5 questions

Each service page lists its deliverables, for example annotated drafts, feedback reports, analysis outputs, documented code or slide decks. Your written scope lists the ones for your project. Files are delivered in editable formats such as Word, Excel, PowerPoint and Python notebooks, with PDF copies where useful.

Yes. Every service includes revision rounds within an agreed window, and changes your supervisor asks for count as revisions. The rounds for each service are listed in the revision policy on the About page and written into your scope.

Errors on our side are corrected at no extra cost, whatever the revision window.

New chapters, additional analyses or experiments, a changed research question or a new target journal are new scope. We quote them separately and only start after you agree, so the original scope and fee are never changed without your say.

You do, once the fees for them are paid. Feedback, analysis outputs, code and other material produced for your project are yours to use, and we do not reuse them for anyone else.

How your research, data and authorship are protected.

6 questions

Yes. Ask for it before you share anything and we will sign it first.

Software, code and what happens with your data.

5 questions

Yes. Our statisticians help you choose appropriate tests, check assumptions, run analyses in SPSS, R, Python, Stata or AMOS, and interpret the output so you can report and defend the results confidently. We also support qualitative analysis in NVivo and ATLAS.ti.

Yes. We help you plan, implement and document data analysis and AI code for your research, from data pipelines to machine-learning experiments. Every script is explained so you can run, modify and defend it.
